KCNC AI Quotation & Estimation

A dedicated RFQ and quotation solution for CNC machine shops — combining structured costing with AI-assisted preparation and engineering review.

Build more consistent, reviewable estimates

Designed to support estimators with manufacturing data and repeatable workflows. AI-generated suggestions require verification against drawings, tolerances, process capabilities and current supplier prices.

01

RFQ Intake

Capture customer enquiries, components, quantities, drawing revisions and requested delivery dates.

02

Drawing-Assisted Review

Assist extraction and organisation of drawing details; confirm critical dimensions, material and tolerances manually.

03

Process & Routing

Plan separate machining operations, work centres, setup allowances and subcontract processes.

04

Cost Estimation

Calculate from configurable machine rates, setup and cycle times, material, tooling, outside processing and overhead assumptions.

05

Quotation Revisions

Maintain a structured estimate and revise commercial assumptions before customer issue.

06

Engineer Approval

Review AI suggestions and validate costs, manufacturability, delivery commitments and quotation terms.

Standalone estimation or a connected workflow

KCNC AI Quotation & Estimation is presented separately from KCNC Manufacturing ERP. Data exchange and integration can be assessed as part of a customer-specific implementation; compatibility is not assumed.

Software functionality, supported drawing formats, AI assistance and automation levels depend on the agreed edition and implementation scope.
