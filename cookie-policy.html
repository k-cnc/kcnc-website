Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used on or around the KCNC website.

Effective date: 8 September 2026

1. KCNC cookie approach

KCNC does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising trackers on this website. Cloudflare Web Analytics, if enabled, is designed to measure website performance without cookies and without collecting visitors’ personal data.

2. Strictly necessary and security technologies

Infrastructure, security, form-processing or external services may use cookies or similar technologies when required to provide their service, protect against abuse, maintain sessions or process a user-requested action.

3. External links

When you follow a link to a CNC manufacturer, CAM supplier, toolholding supplier, probing supplier, WhatsApp or another third-party website, that site may use its own cookies and tracking technologies. Their policies apply after you leave kcnc.in.

4. Browser controls

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies on third-party services may prevent some functions from working.

5. Updates

If KCNC later introduces analytics or marketing technologies that require additional consent in applicable jurisdictions, this policy and the website consent mechanism should be updated accordingly.
