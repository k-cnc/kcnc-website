One operation, one accountable work centre

Define each job operation separately: OP10 Turning on LATHE-01, OP20 VMC Milling on VMC-01, OP30 HMC Machining on HMC-01 and OP40 Inspection. Keep downstream operations waiting until predecessors and required quality checks are complete.

Planning & Routing

Operation number and description, drawing revision, machine/work centre, planned quantities, setup time, standard/CAM cycle time, tooling and fixtures.

Shop-Floor Execution

Start Setup, Setup Complete, Start Production, Pause/Hold with reason, Record Production, Record Reject/Rework, Send to Inspection and Complete Operation.

Readiness & Quality Gates

Material, tooling, fixture, predecessor and quality readiness, with clear waiting/blocked statuses and controlled release to the next operation.
