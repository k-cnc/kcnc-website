Privacy Policy

How KCNC Engineering & Consultancy handles information associated with this website.

1. Information we may process

When you use this website, basic technical information may be processed automatically, including IP address, browser and device information, operating system, approximate country or region, requested pages or paths, referrer information, and date and time of requests.

If you contact KCNC through the website enquiry form, email, telephone or WhatsApp, we may also receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, company, email address, telephone number and details of your enquiry.

2. Why we use this information

Information is used to operate and secure the website, understand website usage and performance, respond to enquiries, prevent abuse or malicious activity, and improve our website and consultancy communications.

3. Analytics, security and service providers

This website uses Cloudflare services for content delivery, performance, analytics and security. Cloudflare may process technical request information in connection with providing those services. Website analytics are used to understand aggregate traffic and site performance; security analytics may include technical request details such as source IP, network information, requested path and user-agent information.

The website enquiry form may use a third-party form-processing service to transmit submitted enquiries to KCNC. Please do not submit confidential, proprietary or sensitive technical information through the public website form; contact KCNC directly when secure handling is required.

4. Data sharing and retention

KCNC does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate, secure and communicate through the website, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. We retain enquiry and technical information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, security, legal or operational purposes.

5. External links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including machine-tool manufacturers and communication services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and KCNC is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

6. Your choices and contact

If you have a privacy question, wish to request correction or deletion of information you have provided to KCNC, or have a concern about how your information is handled, contact us at info@kcnc.in.

7. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated when our website, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
