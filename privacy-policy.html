Privacy Policy

How KCNC handles website enquiries, technical security data and third-party services.

Effective date: 8 September 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how KCNC Engineering & Consultancy (“KCNC”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit www.kcnc.in, contact us, use the website enquiry form, or communicate through email or WhatsApp.

2. Information you provide

You may provide your name, company, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, project requirements and any other information included in an enquiry. Please do not submit confidential drawings, passwords, payment-card information, export-controlled data or other sensitive material through the public website form.

3. Technical and security data

When the website is delivered through Internet infrastructure and security providers, technical data such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser/user-agent information, approximate network location and security-event information may be processed for security, abuse prevention, reliability and troubleshooting. Where Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled, it is designed as privacy-first analytics and does not collect visitors’ personal data.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, evaluate consultancy requirements, prepare proposals, communicate with prospective or existing clients, maintain website security and reliability, and improve our services.

5. Service providers and external services

The website may rely on third-party services including Cloudflare for DNS/security/performance, a website form processor for enquiry delivery, Titan/BigRock email services, GitHub Pages hosting, and WhatsApp when you choose to open a WhatsApp conversation. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Website form

Information submitted through the enquiry form is intended for delivery to info@kcnc.in. If a third-party form-processing service is used, the information passes through that service for transmission. Website form submission does not create a consultancy engagement or confidentiality obligation unless agreed separately in writing.

7. Retention

KCNC keeps business enquiries and related correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, client relationship, record keeping, legal compliance and dispute prevention. Security logs are retained according to the relevant service provider’s settings and plan.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact KCNC to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to legal and legitimate business-record requirements.

9. International processing

Internet, email, hosting, security and messaging providers may process information in countries other than your own. By using those services, information may be transferred according to the provider’s legal terms.

10. Contact

Privacy enquiries: info@kcnc.in.

This policy is general website information and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
